Alfa / Lab I · Before

I — Before

Before the first light,
everything waited.

A horizon is only a promise that something is about to be seen.

II — Within

The sky was never open.
It was an aperture.

One opening decides what the room is allowed to know.

III — Held

Light, once let in,
learns to be a surface.

Held long enough, it stops arriving. It simply stays.

IV — Now

Everything that was waiting is here.
