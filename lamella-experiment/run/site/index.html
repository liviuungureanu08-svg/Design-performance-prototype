01 Presence

4,608 plates. One nervous system.

Every plate is a pivoting blade of anodised bronze with a light‑emitting back. Each one knows where you are, to the centimetre, every eleven milliseconds — so the wall can greet you, write, or render a sun in half‑tones of turned metal.
