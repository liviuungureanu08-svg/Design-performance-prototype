# Concept Search Record — Integrated Surgical Audit 01

Written during the search (19:45–20:05 UTC), before any build. Selection rationale is explicit; there is no hidden score.

## A. Frame (GI Phase A)
**Commercial/experiential problem.** A serious prospect must, within about a minute, (1) feel something exceptional, (2) understand that Gabriel Solutions builds working digital *systems* (web, apps/platforms, AI, automation), not decoration, and (3) recognise their *own* business in it, so the reaction becomes "I want this for mine" rather than "nice work".

**Protected:** Project Truth (scope, UNDERSTAND→DESIGN→BUILD→AMPLIFY, character: sophisticated / intelligent / exclusive / precise / creative / human / rebellious); readable information; accessibility; reduced-motion meaning; no human verdicts.
**Free:** everything else, including the temporal driver (T2), who acts, and medium.
**Forbidden by memory/brief:** hidden-truth reveal as default; imitating prior experiments; dark void / central object / blue line / glass / scroll morph as defaults; sensory overload as the primary experience (VI §30A).

## C. De-correlation (GI Phase C)
- **Hidden premise 1** — F1, F2, F3, F8 all say *"show the system operating on business events"* (operations-centric). Risk: reads as automation SaaS, weak link to "I need him for my **website**".
- **Hidden premise 2** — F4, F5, F6 all say *"the visitor manipulates the design"* (design-centric). Risk: portfolio toy; weak on "system".
- **Hidden premise 3** — F6, F7 are reveals → dropped to the bottom per brief/failure memory.
- Operators applied: *change agency* (viewer → visitor's own hand: F1, F4, F5); *change temporal order* (stream that never stops instead of a scrubber: F1, F2); *make information itself structural* (in F1 the messages are both the image and the content); *reframe the client promise* ("we make your business calmer" → "**we don't make your business quieter, we make everything that arrives go somewhere**" — volume is kept, which is commercially more honest and more desirable).

## E. Selection (GI Phase E — decider)
**Selected: F1 INBOUND.**
1. Breaks the shared premise of all five prior experiments: no scrubber, no hidden B; the image is live from frame 0 and changes only because the visitor acts. (Addresses failure memory: "overuse of delayed reveal", "interactive experiences judged through stills" — agency is the point, so stills cannot stand in for it.)
2. The category is carried by **behaviour on screen**, not explanatory copy (failure memory: "software/digital systems cannot rely only on explanatory copy").
3. Desire mechanism is direct: the stream is a recognisable business's real-sounding requests; "only three of these needed you" is a benefit a prospect can want.
4. The signature moment is describable in one sentence: *"you hold down and a whole business's incoming messages bend out of the pile into order, mid-air; let go and they fall again."*
5. Remove-effect survives (two static states), so static-first discipline can be applied.

**Challenger kept: F5 Constraint Instrument** (design-centric). Why it is not selected: low first-contact impact, highest execution risk (a live design solver that looks credible), and the configurator trope. Why it is kept: if Human Review finds F1 too operations/SaaS-coded for "I need him for my *website*", F5 is the structurally opposite answer. **This identity question (operations-centric vs design-centric) belongs to Human Authority and is left UNRESOLVED.**

**Rejected:** F2 (split halves impact; before/after slider trope; F1 does the same comparison *in time under the visitor's hand* rather than in space). F3 (surveillance feeling contradicts "human"; weak first contact — its useful part, *your own message joins the stream*, is absorbed into F1's DESIRE beat as opt-in). F4 (fails swap tests; physics toy). F6/F7 (reveals; F7 is the trope control). F8 (narrative scrubber = prior premise).

## F. Fixation check
- *Am I re-doing "chaos → order"?* The trope is "messy becomes tidy, once, forever". F1 differs in three named ways: the volume never drops (the stream keeps arriving while ordered); the change is reversible and owned by the visitor (release = it falls again); the end state is not "tidy", it is **"three things need you"** (human). If the build reads as a one-way tidy-up, that is a FAIL to report, not to rationalise.
- *Am I re-doing Exp01 (text → system)?* Shared material: real-sounding client language. Different mechanism: Exp01 reads one brief and constructs surfaces (progress-driven); F1 routes an endless stream (autonomous + input). Text annotation grammar (pen marks, underlines) is **forbidden** in this build to avoid inheriting Exp01's look.
- *Am I re-doing Exp04 ("more than the surface")?* No depth/orbit; no claim that something is behind the page.
- *Am I choosing it because it's buildable?* Partly a risk: canvas text is cheap for me. Counter-check: F5 was rated higher on design-centric identity and I am preserving it as challenger rather than suppressing it.
