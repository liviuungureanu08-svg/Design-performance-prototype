# Human Review Protocol — INBOUND

For the owner (creative authority). About 15 minutes. **Do this before reading any other file in this directory.**
Nothing here states what the system expects you to feel.

## Setup
- Desktop: open `INBOUND_review.html` in a desktop browser, full screen, sound irrelevant.
- Phone: the same file on a phone (or the source served from this directory). Do the desktop pass first.
- Have something to write on. Write answers **before** moving to the next step.

## Pass 1 — first contact (desktop, no instructions)
1. Open the page. Do whatever you would naturally do for up to 60 seconds. Do not read ahead in this protocol.
2. Write, in your own words, your first reaction (one or two sentences).
3. **WOW: yes / no.** (Answer before giving any reason.)
4. What is this company? What does it sell?
5. Did anything happen because of something *you* did? If yes, what?

## Pass 2 — exploration (desktop, ~4 minutes)
6. If you have not already: press and hold anywhere on the field, then let go. Repeat as you like. Try "Keep it running". Try another business at the top.
7. Scroll down through the page and back.
8. Write:
   - What is the one moment you would describe to someone else?
   - What did the colours of the cards mean, if anything?
   - What happens when you let go?
   - Did anything feel forced, generic, confusing or fake? Where?

## Pass 3 — phone (~3 minutes)
9. Same page on a phone. Hold on the field; scroll the page; hold again.
10. Did holding and scrolling ever fight each other? Did anything look broken or crowded?

## Retrospective (next day, without reopening)
11. What do you remember? What was the strongest moment?

## Owner decisions this artifact cannot make (please answer)
