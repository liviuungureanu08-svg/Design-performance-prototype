## What ALFA documents supplied that changed the work
- **GI:** search discipline, de-correlation operators, challenger preservation, stop rule — high practical value.
- **VI:** anti-generic tests, Static-First (and its gap), worst-frame protocol, reduced-motion meaning, conflict priority, responsive priority order — high practical value.
- **Failure memory (06):** directly drove the selection away from reveal/scrubber premises and toward behaviour-carried category meaning — high value.
- **VL:** mid-level structuring vocabulary (see audit) — moderate value, narrower than GI/VI.

## ALFA INTEGRATION SIGNAL: MIXED
GI + VI + failure memory + VL ran together without contradiction and produced a coherent, tested, non-reveal artifact with a truthful trace. But: integration happened inside one model's judgement (no external routing/state), VL overlaps heavily with VI, and the decisive gates (Static, WOW) remain human and open.
